About Us

A new generation of Indian food.

We are building a premium Indian food brand that serves homes, businesses and global buyers with equal care.

Our Story

Rooted in India’s pantry, ready for the world.

India has always been home to some of the world’s most loved foods, from makhana harvested in its wetlands to the dry fruits that fill festive tables.

We started with a simple belief: these foods deserve to be presented, packed and supplied with the same care as any premium brand in the world. Today we are building a business that brings them to every kind of customer, from a family choosing a healthy snack to a distributor planning a new market.

“Premium Indian food, handled with care at every step, from our source to your market.”

Our Vision

To become a trusted global name for premium Indian food.

A brand that customers recognise for quality, and that businesses rely on for consistency, wherever they are in the world.

Our Mission

To make healthy, premium food accessible at every scale.

By selecting carefully, packing thoughtfully and building fair, long-term relationships with customers, retailers, distributors and international buyers.
