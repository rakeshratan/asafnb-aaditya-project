FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can’t find your answer? Send us an enquiry.

Do you supply in bulk / wholesale quantities?

Yes. We welcome wholesale and bulk enquiries for makhana, dry fruits and healthy beverages. Share your products, quantities and delivery location through the Business Enquiry form and we will respond with options.

Can I become a distributor or retail partner?

We are open to partnerships with retailers, wholesalers and distributors. Visit our Distribution page or send a business enquiry with details about your market and network.

Do you work with international buyers?

We are exploring export opportunities and welcome enquiries from importers and international buyers. Requirements vary by destination, so we discuss specifications, packaging and documentation for each order.

Do you offer private label or custom packaging?

Yes, we are open to private-label and custom packaging discussions. Tell us about your brand, product and pack-size requirements in the enquiry form.

Can I order a trial quantity first?

Trial orders can be discussed for new business customers. Select “Trial Order” as the order type in the enquiry form.

How quickly will you respond?

We aim to respond to every enquiry as quickly as possible during business hours. For urgent requirements, contact us on WhatsApp.
